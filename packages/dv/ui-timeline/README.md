# @dv/ui-timeline

English | [中文](README.zh.md)

## Summary

Use this package to give the web application a timeline editor beside the chat. The editor shows one tab per timeline, a viewer playing the selected timeline, a video track whose clips are as wide as they play and marked when stale, and, under it in the order they were added, video tracks whose clips play at their own start times after the main track ends, and sound tracks. You move, trim, split, and remove clips, insert asset-pool assets by dropping them on a track or choosing them with its ＋, and add audio to sound tracks the same ways. The right Sidebar's `dv-timeline` tab type wraps the editor in the project bar; `TimelineView` is the shell's 时间线 / Timeline center view (`dv.center.view`). Both follow the project's current state.

## Use this package

Mount the plugin in a profile that stacks `dsh-web-app` and `@dv/api`, and build the browser bundle first with `pnpm run build`.

The editor also has a toolbar and a ruler. Each rendered clip shows its video's frames along its width: the editor reads one frame per second of the video (at most 40, further apart in a longer video) once per page, and each tile, as wide as the clip is high, shows the frame nearest the time it covers. Until its video's frames are read, a clip repeats its producer's last frame, else its video's first frame. Dragging a clip moves it, and dragging a rendered clip up or down onto the row of a video track moves it to that track at the time it ends on; dragging its edges trims it, the toolbar splits the clip under the playhead, its 撤销 / Undo and 重做 / Redo icon buttons move the project's current position one step back or forward (whichever view made the step) and write no record, 重做 / Redo being disabled while the current position is the last step, and Delete removes the selected clip. The bar under the preview shows the timecode on the left; back 5 s, play or pause, and forward 5 s in the center (J, L, Shift+← and Shift+→ on the focused editor also skip 5 s); and on the right the playback speed button, which cycles 0.5×, 1×, 1.5× and 2× and keeps the speed for the browser tab's session, and full screen. The 原声 / Original audio track draws each clip's waveform over its played range. Dragging the line between the preview and the toolbar (or pressing ArrowUp and ArrowDown on it) changes the track area's height, which the browser remembers under the `localStorage` key `dv-timeline-track-height`; the preview keeps at least 160 px, and the track area keeps at least its toolbar, the ruler and the 视频 / Video track. A line runs under each row of the track area and along the right edge of the track headers. The track area scrolls when its rows do not fit, and its scrollbars show only while it scrolls and for 0.8 s after.

Video tracks and sound tracks sit under the 视频 / Video track, which keeps its clips one after another, and its 原声 / Original audio row, in the order they were added (the timeline's `track_order`). Once there is a video track, the main track is 视频 1 / Video 1 and the video tracks are 视频 2 / Video 2, 视频 3 / Video 3, … in the order they were added. The toolbar's ＋ 添加轨道 / Add track button opens a menu of 视频轨 / Video track and 音轨 / Audio track, each of which writes one `timeline.track_add` that adds an empty track at the bottom; both items are disabled from that write until the refreshed state shows the track. The 轨道层级 / Track layers button beside it lists the video tracks in the order the picture stacks them, the main track first, each with the names of its clips' assets in play order under it, or 空轨道 / Empty track. Each track's up and down arrows swap it with the track above or below it, so its clips show in that row and cover the tracks after it; the top track's up arrow and the bottom track's down arrow are disabled. Between two tracks below the main track, the swap is one `timeline.video_track_move`; between the main track and 视频 2 / Video 2, it is one `timeline.main_track_swap`, which puts the main track's clips on 视频 2 / Video 2 at the times they played and joins 视频 2 / Video 2's clips on the main track without the gaps, and which is disabled while a clip of the main track still renders. The arrows are disabled from the write until the refreshed state shows the move. A press outside the menu or the list, or Escape, closes it. Each video track's header has its speaker button, which mutes the track's clips through `timeline.mute` and dims them, and its delete button. The main track's header has a delete button too, which writes one `timeline.main_track_remove`: 视频 2 / Video 2 becomes the main track, its clips joined in the order of their start times without the gaps between them, and the other video tracks move up one number; the button is disabled while the main track has no clip and there is no video track. A video track's clips sit at their start times, as wide as they play; dragging a clip moves it in time and, by whole rows up or down, to the video track nearest the row it ends on (on a tie, the one in the drag's direction), or, ending nearest the main track's row, into the main track where its middle lands, and dragging its edges trims it, a start trim moving its start along so the rest stays in place. Clicking a clip selects it; Delete or Backspace, or the toolbar's delete button, removes it, and the toolbar's split button splits it at the playhead while the playhead is inside it. Dropping a video on a track adds it there at the drop point; the ＋ of a track opens the same asset picker as the ＋ of the main track and adds the chosen video after the track's last clip, or at the playhead on an empty track. The main track covers the video tracks up to its end; after it, the preview shows and plays the clip of the video track with the lowest number there. Its time runs to the end of the latest clip of any video track, and past the main track's end it moves by the clock.

Sound tracks sit under the 原声 / Original audio track. Each track's header shows its name, 声音 {n} / Sound {n} until it has one, and its buttons on a line under the name. Double-clicking the header, or 重命名 / Rename in its right-click menu, opens a name box that Enter or leaving the box saves and Escape cancels; 删除音轨 / Delete track in the same menu, or the delete button on the header, deletes the track with its sounds; 声音 1 / Sound 1 of a new timeline can be deleted too, leaving no sound track. The header's speaker button mutes or unmutes the track through `timeline.mute`, and the preview plays a muted track's sounds silent and dims them; the speaker button on the 视频 / Video header does the same for the clips' own audio and dims the 原声 / Original audio track, and an export of a timeline with the clips' own audio muted has no audio. The speaker buttons are disabled while the project has no timeline. 音轨 / Audio track in the toolbar's ＋ 添加轨道 / Add track menu adds an empty sound track. Every track's ＋ sits in its header, right of the delete button. The ＋ of each sound track opens the 添加声音 / Add sound dialog, which shows where the new sound starts and lists 上传本地音频 / Upload local audio and the project's audio assets, newest first; uploading imports the chosen file into the asset pool and then adds it. A new sound starts where the last sound of its track ends, or at the playhead on an empty track. Dropping an audio asset on a sound track adds it there at the drop point. From a sound add until the new sound shows, the ＋ buttons of the sound tracks are disabled and a dropped sound adds nothing. Each sound is a block as wide as it plays, with its file name in a strip above the waveform of the part of its asset it plays. Dragging a sound moves it in time and, by whole rows up or down, to the sound track nearest the row it ends on (on a tie, the one in the drag's direction); dragging its edges trims it, a start trim moving its start along so the rest stays in place, down to the timeline's start and up to the asset's end. Clicking a sound selects it; Delete or Backspace, the toolbar's 删除声音 / Delete sound button, or 删除 / Delete in its right-click menu removes it, and the toolbar's split button or 在播放头处拆分 / Split at playhead in its menu splits it at the playhead while the playhead is inside it. While the preview plays, each sound plays in step with the picture at the playback speed, from its in point. While the timeline has sounds, the toolbar shows 导出暂不包含声音 / Export does not include sounds yet beside Export.

A right click in the track area opens a menu at the pointer instead of the browser's. On a clip of any video track it offers 在播放头处拆分 / Split at playhead, enabled while the playhead is inside the clip, and 删除 / Delete; on a video track's header, 静音 / Mute or 取消静音 / Unmute, 上移 / Move up and 下移 / Move down, as the arrows of 轨道层级 / Track layers, and 删除视频轨 / Delete video track; on a sound track's header, the mute item, 重命名 / Rename and 删除音轨 / Delete track; on the 原声 / Original audio row, the mute item of the clips' own audio; and on a sound, 在播放头处拆分 / Split at playhead and 删除 / Delete. On a free spot of a lane it offers 在这里添加视频 / Add a video here or 在这里添加声音 / Add a sound here, which opens the asset picker, titled 选一个素材放到 {time} / Pick an asset to place at {time}, or the 添加声音 / Add sound dialog to add at the time under the pointer, and 添加视频轨 / Add video track and 添加音轨 / Add audio track, which add an empty track at the bottom. On the main track, the picked video goes in before the clip whose middle is after that time. Each item writes what the matching button writes and is disabled while that button is. A press outside the menu or Escape closes it, and a right press on a clip starts no drag.

The Host half registers nothing. The browser half registers the `dv-timeline` tab type (offered by the Sidebar's guide page as "Timeline"), the `dvTimeline` locale namespace in Chinese and English, and the tab body under its own id `@dv/ui-timeline`. While the center service `dvCenter` of `@dv/ui-shell` is present, it registers `TimelineView` as the `dv.center.view` entry `timeline` (order 1, labeled with the tab type's label 时间线 / Timeline), which edits the project of the center's owner prop `projectId`, writes for the chat session of its owner prop `session`, and applies its owner prop `request`. In the same scope it registers the entry `dv.timeline.insert` of the `dv.media.preview.action` slot, a button of the asset preview that `dvMedia.preview` of `@dv/ui-media` opens: the button 插入片段 / Insert clip shows for a video asset of the open project, and clicking it calls `dvCenter.show({ view: 'timeline', target: { insert } })` with the asset, which the open project's timeline view inserts, and closes the preview. While the canvas service `dvCanvas` of `@dv/ui-canvas` is present, it also registers a `RecordsInUse` of `@dv/ui-canvas/contract` in `dvCanvas.recordsInUse` under the same id: its `uses` lists, for each clip of every timeline, the render the clip waits for (`source.record`), else the producer of the clip's asset, and the producer of the asset of each clip of the video tracks under the 视频 track, so the canvas draws the takes that the timelines play; its `badges` puts 已裁剪 / Trimmed on the asset of each clip with an in or out point. In the same scope it registers the node type `timeline` in `dvCanvas.nodeTypes`, for the nodes that `timeline.place` of [`@dv/timeline`](../timeline/README.md) makes, and the section 时间线 / Timelines (order 1) in `dvCanvas.addMenu`, which lists every timeline of the project by its shown name and puts a picked one on the canvas with one `timeline.place` record; a timeline that a node already shows names that node. A timeline node's title is the timeline's name, its meta the timeline's length such as `7.0s`, and its card, the `dv.canvas.card` entry `timeline`, holds `TimelineEditor` for that timeline with its `card` option: no timeline tabs and no track-area resize, the toolbar ending with 打开编辑器 / Open editor, which shows the timeline in the 时间线 view, and, while the node is selected, the editor's preview above the node, as wide as the node at the first ready clip's aspect ratio (16:9 when its size is unknown), playing in step with the editor's playhead. The preview grows with the node in both directions: it is as wide as the node, or, when the node is taller than the default 640 × 390 proportions give, height × 640 / 390 wide and centered over the node; under its picture a progress bar shows the playhead, and a press on it moves the playhead there. The editor and the preview draw their text and controls larger as the node grows, by the smaller of width / 480 and height / 290 and at least 1, so a default node draws them at 4 / 3 of the panel's size and a node enlarged in both directions draws them larger still. A press on the editor's controls (its buttons and slider, ruler, clips, sounds, track headers, and the 原声 / Original audio row) selects the node and stays with the editor; a press anywhere else in the card, the preview included, moves the node, and a click there selects it. The editor keeps its keys, such as Delete on a selected clip, and its own right-click menus; a right click elsewhere opens the canvas's node menu. Its menus open inside the editor, it divides pointer distances by the canvas zoom, and its track area scrolls both ways by wheel or touchpad without showing a scrollbar. A refused write shows its error above the editor until the next write succeeds. Its `uses` names the assets of the clips of every video track and of the sounds, and the render of each placeholder clip, so the canvas draws a line from each of their nodes and, while the timeline node is selected, outlines them with a glow and fades the other nodes. A double click calls `dvCenter.show({ view: 'timeline', target: { timeline, clip: null } })` while `dvCenter` is present. A node whose timeline was deleted shows 这条时间线已删除 / This timeline was deleted, and one without clips 时间线还没有片段 / The timeline has no clips yet. While the History service `dvHistory` of `@dv/ui-history` is present, it registers in `dvHistory.rows` the `HistoryRow` `timeline.` of `@dv/ui-history/contract`: it adds no subject to the label of a timeline record, and its `focus` returns the center target `{ view: 'timeline', target: { timeline, clip } }` with the record's timeline and clip. The timeline is the record's `timeline` param, else the timeline of its `clip` param (each clip belongs to the timeline of the record that assigned it; a split adds to the split clip's timeline), else `t1` for a `timeline.create` without one; the clip is the `clip` param, else the first clip the record assigned. A record whose timeline is not found focuses nothing. While `@dv/ui-composer` provides `dvMentions`, it also registers the mention kind `clip` (ID `@dv/ui-timeline`, section 片段 / Clips, order 0, so the `@` menu lists clips before every other kind): each clip of each timeline as 时间线 1 · 片段 2 / Timeline 1 · Clip 2 (for an unnamed timeline), with the first 40 characters of the prompt of the record that made the clip's asset (for a placeholder clip, the render it waits for), and as its thumbnail that record's image output, else the clip's asset. `TimelineView` takes each request that the center hands it once it applied the request. A request `{ timeline, clip }` selects that timeline, and with a `clip`, the editor selects that clip and moves the playhead to its start once it shows the timeline. A request `{ insert }` inserts the asset once the view has read the state, as `timeline.clip_insert` at the end of the selected timeline (else the first one), or as `timeline.create` of `t1` holding the clip when the state has no timeline, and selects `t1`. A request of neither form changes nothing.

Every record carries `surface: 'timeline'`, the chat session the view sits beside (stored as the record's `session`; the record is a step after the project's current position), and an intent in the DSH interface language naming the gesture. `TimelineView` shows the timeline that the open project's center field `timeline` names, else the first timeline, and a timeline that the user picks or creates there sets the field through the owner prop `note`, so the shell keeps it in the URL and the session switcher's cover follows it. The tab body keeps its own selected timeline per project, which leaves the center alone. `TimelineView` follows `<html lang>` through `@dv/ui-kit/locale.ts`; switching timeline tabs stops playback and resets the viewer and playhead. Clicking a clip selects it and sends nothing. The asset picker and the 添加声音 / Add sound dialog open to the right of the ＋ that opened them, level with its row, or where a lane menu was opened; from the lower half of the track area they open upward, and they stay inside it. The ＋ picker takes focus when it opens. Its close button, Escape while focus is in the picker, on ＋, or on an element that contains the picker, a press outside it, or tabbing out of it closes it and inserts nothing. The 添加声音 / Add sound dialog takes focus and closes the same ways, adding nothing. A clip whose asset reports no duration is drawn as five seconds; a sound whose asset reports no duration is drawn as long as its `<audio>` element measures, else five seconds. The tracks reach to the end of the last clip or sound, whichever is later.

The DOM carries test IDs `dv-timeline-body`, `dv-timeline-editor`, `dv-timeline-viewer`, `dv-timeline-viewer-empty`, `dv-timeline-time`, `dv-timeline-exported`, `dv-timeline-export-no-sounds`, `dv-timeline-ruler`, `dv-timeline-track-area` and `dv-timeline-playhead`; each tile of a clip's frames carries `data-filmstrip-frame` (the frame's number), each clip carries `data-clip` (its clip ID), `data-clip-position` and `data-clip-stale`, each sound block and its `<audio>` element carry `data-sound` (its sound ID), each sound track carries `data-sound-track` and its header `data-sound-track-head` (the track number), each video track under the 视频 track carries `data-video-track` and its header `data-video-track-head`, the 视频 track's header carries `data-main-track-head`, the 原声 row's header and lane `data-audio-head` and `data-audio-lane`, each of its clips `data-track-clip-block` (its clip ID) and the clip's `<video>` element in the preview `data-track-clip`, and each tab carries `data-timeline`.

## Understand the implementation

<details>
<summary>Implementation internals — click to expand</summary>

`TimelineBody` holds the view session from `@dv/ui-kit/useView.ts` and renders the project bar above `TimelineEditor`. `TimelineView` reads the project's current state itself. `placeTimeline` turns one timeline of the state into positioned clips; `useTimelinePlayer` plays them through two stacked `<video>` elements. The waveforms of the 原声 track and of the sound blocks come from `clipPeaks` and `useAudioEnvelope` of `@dv/ui-kit/waveform.ts`. `placeSounds` positions the sounds of the shown timeline; each sound has a hidden `<audio>` element, and `useSoundPlayback` plays, pauses, and seeks each element when the player's position, playing state, or speed changes, seeking again when the element is more than 0.25 s from the playhead. `placeTrackClips` positions the clips of the video tracks under the 视频 track, `topTrackClipAt` picks the one the preview shows after the 视频 track's end, and `trackRows` turns the timeline's `track_order` into the rows under the 原声 row; each has a `<video>` element in the preview frame, which `useTrackClipPlayback` keeps at the playhead the same way and which plays muted unless its clip shows. Past the 视频 track's last clip, `useTimelinePlayer` moves the playhead by the clock up to the end of the latest track clip. Each gesture is one `DvClient.runOperation` call followed by a state refetch.

## Model Experience

Indirectly, through `@dv/project`; the records its gestures write reach the model only through the project state and update messages and the `dv_proj_*` and operation tools of [`@dv/project`](../project/README.md).

#### KV Cache effect

None; the timeline sends nothing to a model.

## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

<a id="known-limitations-and-deferred-work"></a>

- **One audio row** — the 原声 (original audio) track mirrors only the 视频 track's clips and has no edits of its own; the clips of the video tracks have no audio row, and subtitles have no lane.
- **Export has no sounds** — `timeline.export` of `@dv/timeline` leaves out every sound; the toolbar note says so.
- **Sounds play only with the picture** — a sound plays only while the preview plays the clips: a timeline without a ready clip on the 视频 track or a clip on a video track cannot play, and a sound that runs past the last clip of every video track stops there.
- **Sounds cannot be edited in place** — a sound cannot be dragged, trimmed, moved to another track, or given a volume.
- **Waveforms decode in the browser** — the 原声 track draws each clip's waveform, and a sound block its sound's waveform, (`@dv/ui-kit/waveform.ts`) by downloading the clip's whole media file and decoding its audio with the Web Audio API, one asset at a time and cached in memory per page load; a clip keeps a plain block while its audio decodes, when decoding fails, and when the media has no audio track. When the media file cannot be fetched (a network error or a 5xx status), the result is not cached: the clip asks again after 2, 8, and 30 seconds, and a later mount of the clip starts over.
