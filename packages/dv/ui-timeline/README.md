# @dv/ui-timeline

English | [中文](README.zh.md)

## Summary

Use this package to give the web application a timeline editor beside the chat. It shows one tab per timeline, a preview, and the selected timeline's video and sound tracks. Every video track holds clips at their start times; the preview shows the topmost track's clip at the playhead. You move, trim, split, and remove clips and sounds, and add asset-pool assets by a drop or a track's ＋. The right Sidebar's `dv-timeline` tab type wraps the editor in the project bar; `TimelineView` is the shell's 时间线 / Timeline center view (`dv.center.view`). Both follow the project's current state.

## Use this package

Mount the plugin in a profile that stacks `dsh-web-app` and `@dv/api`, and build the browser bundle first with `pnpm run build`.

The editor also has a toolbar and a ruler. Each clip with an asset shows its video's frames along its width: the editor reads one frame per second of the video (at most 40, further apart in a longer video) once per page, and each tile, as high as the clip and as wide as the video's aspect ratio gives, shows the frame nearest the time it covers. Until its video's frames are read, a clip repeats the image output of the record that made its video, else its video's first frame. A placeholder clip, whose render is still running or failed, is a striped block as wide as the render's planned length that shows 渲染中… / Rendering… or 渲染失败 / Render failed. While a clip is a placeholder, 导出 / Export is disabled and the toolbar shows 还有 {count} 个片段没就绪，全部就绪后才能导出 / Export waits until every clip is ready ({count} still rendering) beside it. The toolbar's 撤销 / Undo and 重做 / Redo icon buttons move the project's current position one step back or forward (whichever view made the step) and write no record, 重做 / Redo being disabled while the current position is the last step. The bar under the preview shows the timecode on the left; back 5 s, play or pause, and forward 5 s in the center (J, L, Shift+← and Shift+→ on the focused editor also skip 5 s, and Space plays or pauses); and on the right the playback speed button, which cycles 0.5×, 1×, 1.5× and 2× and keeps the speed for the browser tab's session, and full screen. Dragging the line between the preview and the toolbar (or pressing ArrowUp and ArrowDown on it) changes the track area's height, which the browser remembers under the `localStorage` key `dv-timeline-track-height`; the preview keeps at least 160 px, and the track area keeps at least its toolbar, the ruler and the first track row. A line runs under each row of the track area and along the right edge of the track headers. The track area scrolls when its rows do not fit, and its scrollbars show only while it scrolls and for 0.8 s after.

Every video track works the same way. A timeline with one video track names it 视频 / Video; with more, they are 视频 1 / Video 1, 视频 2 / Video 2, … from the top. A video clip's own audio has no row of its own: it plays with the clip, and the track's header mutes it. The rows of the video tracks and sound tracks keep the order the tracks were added in (the timeline's `track_order`); a new timeline has one video track above one sound track. The toolbar's ＋ 添加轨道 / Add track button opens a menu of 视频轨 / Video track and 音轨 / Audio track, each of which writes one `timeline.track_add` that adds an empty track at the bottom; both items are disabled from that write until the refreshed state shows the track. The 轨道层级 / Track layers button beside it lists the video tracks from the top, each with the names of its clips' assets in play order under it, or 空轨道 / Empty track. Each track's up and down arrows write one `timeline.video_track_move` that swaps it with the track above or below it, so the clips of each of the two tracks show in the other's row; the top track's up arrow and the bottom track's down arrow are disabled, and every arrow is disabled from the write until the refreshed state shows the move. A press outside the menu or the list, or Escape, closes it.

Where a video track has a clip, the clip covers every video track under it, and a gap in a track shows the track under it. Each video track's header has three buttons on a line under its name. The speaker button mutes or unmutes the own audio of the track's clips through `timeline.mute`; a muted track's clips are dimmed, the preview plays its clips silent, and an export leaves their audio out. The delete button writes one `timeline.video_track_remove`, which deletes the track with its clips, and the video tracks under it move up one number. ＋ opens the asset picker and adds the picked video after the track's last clip, or at the playhead on an empty track. While a mute, a track delete, a track move or a track add runs, and until the refreshed state shows it, the speaker and delete buttons of every track are disabled; every header button is disabled while the project has no timeline.

A clip sits at its start time, as wide as it plays. Dragging a clip moves it in time and to the video track nearest the row the drag ends on (on a tie, the one in the drag's direction); a placeholder clip moves the same way, and the moved clip is selected once the move succeeds. Dragging an edge of a clip with an asset trims it, a start trim moving its start along so the rest stays in place; a placeholder clip has no trim handles. Clicking a clip selects it, and one clip or one sound is selected at a time. Delete or Backspace, or the toolbar's 删除片段 / Delete clip button, removes the selected clip. The toolbar's split button splits the selected clip at the playhead, or with nothing selected the clip the preview shows there; it is enabled only while that clip has its asset and the playhead is inside it, more than 0.05 s from each end. Dropping a video from the asset pool on a video track adds it to that track at the drop point. No edit moves another clip, so nothing closes a gap; when an add, a move or a trim would overlap another clip of the same track, the server refuses the write, and the refusal's reason shows above the editor until the next write succeeds.

The preview shows and plays the clip of the video track with the lowest number at the playhead, silent while its track is muted. Each clip with an asset has its own `<video>` element in the preview; the elements of the other clips stay paused and hidden. Where the clip on top is a placeholder, the preview shows its status, such as 视频 上的片段：渲染中… / Clip on Video: Rendering…, and where no clip plays the preview has no picture. The playhead follows a clock at the playback speed up to the end of the last clip of any video track, and waits while the clip the preview shows is still loading. At that end the clip that ends there stays shown at its last frame. A clip's element loads its whole file only when the clips of tracks with lower numbers do not cover all of the clip and the clip is under the playhead or starts within 5 s after it; any other element loads only its metadata.

Each sound track's header shows its name, 声音 {n} / Sound {n} until it has one, and its buttons on a line under the name. Double-clicking the header, or 重命名 / Rename in its right-click menu, opens a name box that Enter or leaving the box saves and Escape cancels; 删除音轨 / Delete track in the same menu, or the delete button on the header, deletes the track with its sounds; 声音 1 / Sound 1 of a new timeline can be deleted too, leaving no sound track. The header's speaker button mutes or unmutes the track through `timeline.mute`, and the preview plays a muted track's sounds silent and dims them. The speaker buttons are disabled while the project has no timeline. 音轨 / Audio track in the toolbar's ＋ 添加轨道 / Add track menu adds an empty sound track. Every track's ＋ sits in its header, right of the delete button. The ＋ of each sound track opens the 添加声音 / Add sound dialog, which shows where the new sound starts and lists 上传本地音频 / Upload local audio and the project's audio assets, newest first; uploading imports the chosen file into the asset pool and then adds it. A new sound starts where the last sound of its track ends, or at the playhead on an empty track. Dropping an audio asset on a sound track adds it there at the drop point. From a sound add until the sound that its record names shows, the ＋ buttons of the sound tracks are disabled and a dropped sound adds nothing; they are enabled at once when the add fails, its record names no sound, or the editor switches to another timeline. Each sound is a block as wide as it plays, with its file name in a strip above the waveform of the part of its asset it plays. Dragging a sound moves it in time and, by whole rows up or down, to the sound track nearest the row it ends on (on a tie, the one in the drag's direction); dragging its edges trims it, a start trim moving its start along so the rest stays in place, down to the timeline's start and up to the asset's end. Clicking a sound selects it; Delete or Backspace, the toolbar's 删除声音 / Delete sound button, or 删除 / Delete in its right-click menu removes it, and the toolbar's split button or 在播放头处拆分 / Split at playhead in its menu splits it at the playhead while the playhead is inside it. While the preview plays, each sound plays in step with the picture at the playback speed, from its in point. While the timeline has sounds, the toolbar shows 导出暂不包含声音 / Export does not include sounds yet beside Export.

A right click in the track area opens a menu at the pointer instead of the browser's. On a clip it selects the clip and offers 在播放头处拆分 / Split at playhead, enabled while the clip has its asset and the playhead is inside it, and 删除 / Delete; on a video track's header, 静音 / Mute or 取消静音 / Unmute, 上移 / Move up and 下移 / Move down, as the arrows of 轨道层级 / Track layers, and 删除视频轨 / Delete video track; on a sound track's header, the mute item, 重命名 / Rename and 删除音轨 / Delete track; and on a sound, 在播放头处拆分 / Split at playhead and 删除 / Delete. On a free spot of a lane it offers 在这里添加视频 / Add a video here or 在这里添加声音 / Add a sound here, which opens the asset picker, titled 选一个素材放到 {time} / Pick an asset to place at {time}, or the 添加声音 / Add sound dialog to add at the time under the pointer, and 添加视频轨 / Add video track and 添加音轨 / Add audio track, which add an empty track at the bottom. Each item writes what the matching button writes and is disabled while that button is. A press outside the menu or Escape closes it, and a right press on a clip starts no drag.

The Host half registers nothing. The browser half registers the `dv-timeline` tab type (offered by the Sidebar's guide page as "Timeline"), the `dvTimeline` locale namespace in Chinese and English, and the tab body under its own id `@dv/ui-timeline`. While the center service `dvCenter` of `@dv/ui-shell` is present, it registers `TimelineView` as the `dv.center.view` entry `timeline` (order 1, labeled with the tab type's label 时间线 / Timeline), which edits the project of the center's owner prop `projectId`, writes for the chat session of its owner prop `session`, and applies its owner prop `request`. In the same scope it registers the entry `dv.timeline.insert` of the `dv.media.preview.action` slot, a button of the asset preview that `dvMedia.preview` of `@dv/ui-media` opens: the button 插入片段 / Insert clip shows for a video asset of the open project, and clicking it calls `dvCenter.show({ view: 'timeline', target: { insert } })` with the asset, which the open project's timeline view inserts, and closes the preview. While the canvas service `dvCanvas` of `@dv/ui-canvas` is present, it also registers a `RecordsInUse` of `@dv/ui-canvas/contract` in `dvCanvas.recordsInUse` under the same id: its `uses` lists, for each clip of every video track of every timeline, the render the clip waits for or was laid out from (`source.record`), else the producer of the clip's asset, so the canvas draws the takes that the timelines play; its `badges` puts 已裁剪 / Trimmed on the asset of each clip with an in or out point. In the same scope it registers the node type `timeline` in `dvCanvas.nodeTypes`, for the nodes that `timeline.place` of [`@dv/timeline`](../timeline/README.md) makes, and the section 时间线 / Timelines (order 1) in `dvCanvas.addMenu`, which lists every timeline of the project by its shown name and puts a picked one on the canvas with one `timeline.place` record; a timeline that a node already shows names that node. A timeline node's title is the timeline's name, its meta the timeline's length such as `7.0s`, and its card, the `dv.canvas.card` entry `timeline`, holds `TimelineEditor` for that timeline with its `card` option: no timeline tabs and no track-area resize, the toolbar ending with 打开编辑器 / Open editor, which shows the timeline in the 时间线 view, and, while the node is selected, the editor's preview above the node, as wide as the node at the aspect ratio of the earliest clip with an asset on the topmost video track that has one (16:9 when its size is unknown), playing in step with the editor's playhead. The preview grows with the node in both directions: it is as wide as the node, or, when the node is taller than the default 640 × 390 proportions give, height × 640 / 390 wide and centered over the node; under its picture a progress bar shows the playhead, and a press on it moves the playhead there. The editor and the preview draw their text and controls larger as the node grows, by the smaller of width / 480 and height / 290 and at least 1, so a default node draws them at 4 / 3 of the panel's size and a node enlarged in both directions draws them larger still. A press on the editor's controls (its buttons and slider, ruler, clips, sounds, and track headers) selects the node and stays with the editor; a press anywhere else in the card, the preview included, moves the node, and a click there selects it. The editor keeps its keys, such as Delete on a selected clip, and its own right-click menus; a right click elsewhere opens the canvas's node menu. Its menus open inside the editor, it divides pointer distances by the canvas zoom, and its track area scrolls both ways by wheel or touchpad without showing a scrollbar. A refused write shows its error above the editor until the next write succeeds. Its `uses` names the assets of the clips of every video track and of the sounds, and the render of each placeholder clip, so the canvas draws a line from each of their nodes and, while the timeline node is selected, outlines them with a glow and fades the other nodes. A double click calls `dvCenter.show({ view: 'timeline', target: { timeline, clip: null } })` while `dvCenter` is present. A node whose timeline was deleted shows 这条时间线已删除 / This timeline was deleted, and the preview of a timeline without clips shows 这条时间线还没有片段，从素材库拖入或点 ＋ 插入 / This timeline has no clips yet. Drag one in from the asset pool or click ＋. While the History service `dvHistory` of `@dv/ui-history` is present, it registers in `dvHistory.rows` the `HistoryRow` `timeline.` of `@dv/ui-history/contract`: it adds no subject to the label of a timeline record, and its `focus` returns the center target `{ view: 'timeline', target: { timeline, clip } }` with the record's timeline and clip. The timeline is the record's `timeline` param, else the timeline of its `clip` or `sound` param (each clip and each sound belongs to the timeline of the record that assigned it; a clip split adds to the split clip's timeline), else `t1` for a `timeline.create` without one; the clip is the `clip` param, else the first clip the record assigned. A record whose timeline is not found focuses nothing. While `@dv/ui-composer` provides `dvMentions`, it also registers the mention kind `clip` (ID `@dv/ui-timeline`, section 片段 / Clips, order 0, so the `@` menu lists clips before every other kind): each clip of each timeline, numbered by start time on its video track, as 时间线 1 · 片段 2 / Timeline 1 · Clip 2 (for an unnamed timeline) on track 0 and as 时间线 1 · 视频 2 · 片段 1 / Timeline 1 · Video 2 · Clip 1 on another video track, with the first 40 characters of the prompt of the record that made the clip's asset (for a placeholder clip, the render it waits for), and as its thumbnail that record's image output, else the clip's asset. `TimelineView` takes each request that the center hands it once it applied the request. A request `{ timeline, clip }` selects that timeline, and with a `clip`, the editor selects that clip and moves the playhead to its start once it shows the timeline. A request `{ insert }` inserts the asset once the view has read the state: as `timeline.clip_add` on track 0 of the selected timeline (else the first one), where that track's last clip ends, or, when the state has no timeline, as `timeline.create` of `t1` holding the clip, which the view then selects. A request of neither form changes nothing.

## Understand the implementation

<details>
<summary>Implementation internals — click to expand</summary>

`TimelineBody` holds the view session from `@dv/ui-kit/useView.ts` and renders the project bar above `TimelineEditor`. `TimelineView` reads the project's current state itself. `placeClips` places the clips of one timeline as the reducer of `@dv/timeline` does: each clip plays from its `start_sec`, or from where the clip before it on its track ends when that is later; a placeholder clip takes the planned length of the render it waits for, and a clip whose asset length the state does not hold takes `FALLBACK_CLIP_SECONDS` of `@dv/ui-kit/timeline.ts`. `topClipAt` picks the clip that the preview shows at a time, the one on the track with the lowest number, and `trackRows` turns the timeline's `track_order` into the rows of the track area. Each clip with an asset has a `<video data-track-clip>` element in the preview frame. `useTrackClipPlayback` keeps the element of the shown clip at the playhead, paused while the player is paused and else playing at the playback speed, and seeks it again when it is more than 0.25 s from the playhead; it keeps the other elements paused. `trackClipPreload` lets an element load its whole file only when the clips of tracks with lower numbers do not cover all of its clip (`coveredClips`, which the editor works out once per change of the clips) and the clip is under the playhead or starts within 5 s after it. `useTimelinePlayer(timelineId, span, rate, stalled)` moves the playhead by a clock at the playback speed up to `span`, the end of the last clip of any track, and holds it while `stalled` returns true; the editor passes a function that calls `clipStalled` on the shown clip's element, which is true while the browser loads the file from the network and has no data past the current frame. The waveforms of the sound blocks come from `clipPeaks` and `useAudioEnvelope` of `@dv/ui-kit/waveform.ts`. `placeSounds` positions the sounds of the shown timeline; each sound has a hidden `<audio>` element, and `useSoundPlayback` plays, pauses, and seeks each element when the player's position, playing state, or speed changes, seeking again when the element is more than 0.25 s from the playhead. Each edit is one `DvClient.runOperation` call, and the tab body and the center view refetch the state after it.

## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

<a id="known-limitations-and-deferred-work"></a>

- **A clip's own audio is not drawn** — a video clip's own audio plays with the clip and its track's header mutes it, but no row shows its waveform or takes edits; subtitles have no lane.
- **Export has no sounds** — `timeline.export` of `@dv/timeline` leaves out every sound; the toolbar note says so.
- **Sounds play only with the picture** — the preview plays only while a clip of a video track is ready, and only up to the end of the last clip of any video track: a timeline without a ready clip cannot play, and a sound that runs past that end stops there.
- **Sounds have no volume control** — the preview plays every sound at full volume; a sound cannot be given a volume or a fade.
- **Clips do not snap, and an overlap is refused after the drop** — a dragged clip does not snap to its neighbors; a drag that would overlap another clip of its track is sent as it is and refused by the server, and the reason shows above the editor.
- **Waveforms decode in the browser** — a sound block draws its sound's waveform (`@dv/ui-kit/waveform.ts`) by downloading the sound's whole media file and decoding its audio with the Web Audio API, one asset at a time and cached in memory per page load; a sound keeps a plain block while its audio decodes, when decoding fails, and when the media has no audio track. When the media file cannot be fetched (a network error or a 5xx status), the result is not cached: the sound asks again after 2, 8, and 30 seconds, and a later mount of the sound starts over.
