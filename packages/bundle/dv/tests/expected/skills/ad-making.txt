# Ad making

An ad has one cut, a 5 to 15 s film, per aspect ratio; the video model draws the copy and makes the sound. The project block in your system prompt is the current state; read it before every call, and pass `reason` wherever a tool requires it. Make every picture and sound with this project's tools; for anything else (last section), say so once, offer the nearest thing, and add nothing unasked.

## When to use it

The user wants an ad or product film of a physical product from a product photo. Not for a talking-head ad, an edit of existing clips, or a screen demo.

## 1. Product and model

When the project block names no project, call `dv_proj_create` with `title`. A file the user attaches becomes a project asset. With no product photo, ask for one and stop.

If `dv_inspect_image` is in your tool list, call it with `inputs.image` = the photo's asset ID and `question` = "Product, body color, material, moving parts; is the photo sharp?"; otherwise read these facts from the photo in the user's message.

Say the facts in one line. When the photo cannot show the product, say what to reshoot and stop.

Use a `ref2v` video model that renders sound, such as `fasth3` with the provider that the model catalog lists for it, and the photo as its reference.

If `dv_models_get` is in your tool list, call it with `modality: video`, `task: ref2v`, `provider` and `model`, and offer only the durations and aspect ratios it lists; otherwise offer 5, 10 or 15 s, and `dv_story_preview` names any value the model refuses.

## 2. Brief and three storylines (one stop)

Reply with:

- One line of defaults: main variant and color; 10 s (or 5 or 15); 16:9 (each extra ratio renders again); the look (white studio, dark rim light, brand color field, lifestyle scene); no spoken line.
- Three storylines in words, three different films. Fit these spines to the product: launch (hero view, detail, the product's own action, closing), feature touch (still, trigger, action, detail, result), color family (main variant, the others enter, the set closes). Each gives its name, the first 2 seconds exactly, and its beats, look and sound in one line each. Put your recommendation first with its reason.
- Two or three copy lines: English, 3 to 5 words, at most 32 characters, no slogan. Translate a line the user gave in Chinese.

End the turn with one question in bold, such as **Which storyline and copy line, and do the defaults hold?** Build nothing before the user picks. On "you decide" or "no stops", take your recommendation, name the other two in one line each, and go on.

## 3. Spec with numbers

Write the spec in your reply and repeat its numbers in every prompt:

- BEATS: 3 to 4 for 5 s, 5 to 7 for 10 s, 6 to 9 for 15 s, each a time range with one real action of this product (the lid opens 30 degrees). The last 2 s hold the product and the copy.
- LOOK: background hex, key light direction, the product at least 40% of the frame height in the hero beat, its body color and finish unchanged.
- COPY: at most twice in 10 s, one line at a time in the middle band, held at least 0.3 s a word plus 0.5 s; first half dark gray (white on a dark look), second half the product's color by name. Never write arrows, slashes or plus signs; the model draws them.
- SOUND: about 100 BPM, plucks, a soft kick, no vocals, a hard stop on the last beat; or the spoken line.
- 9:16: recompose, never squeeze; the product and the copy stay between 9% and 69% of the height from the top.
- BANNED: split screens, panels, grids; mirror floors; glow, particles, flares; invented interfaces, numbers or claims; a logo the user did not give; a recolored product. Add each default a review finds.

## 4. Spoken line (only when asked)

The spoken line sets the shortest shot length; a line too long for the model gets fewer words.

If `dv_audio_generate` is in your tool list, load the prompt skill of its speech model, call it with `modality: audio`, `task: t2a`, `provider`, `model` and `prompt` = the line in that skill's markup, and keep `duration_sec` at least the voice clip's `duration_sec` rounded up plus 1 s; otherwise write the line as dialogue in the prompt and count 0.4 s a word (4 Chinese characters a second) plus 1 s.

You cannot hear a voice clip; the user picks the take in step 6.

## 5. One plan per aspect ratio

Load the prompt skill of the video model and task, and follow it.

1. Per aspect ratio, `dv_plan_create` with `title` (such as "Mug ad 9:16"), `aspect_ratio`, `references` (the photo's asset ID, and any voice clip's) and `shots`: one shot with `modality`, `task`, `provider`, `model`, `duration_sec` and `prompt`.
2. The prompt carries every beat, cites the photo and the voice clip as `@[<label>](dv:asset/<AssetId>)`, spells out each copy line word for word with its time window and colors, and ends with the BANNED line. A prompt missing a copy line is not ready.

## 6. Preview and ask (one stop)

1. `dv_story_preview` with `plan` for each plan. When `problems` is not empty, or the user changes the plan later, update it with `dv_plan_update`: `plan` and the complete plan (`title`, `aspect_ratio`, `references`, every shot in `shots`); then preview again.
2. Reply with the spec, a beat table (time, picture, camera, copy, sound), one line per plan (ratio, length, `version`), and the voice clip links. End the turn with one question in bold, such as **Render both cuts now?** Do not call `dv_story_approve` in this turn.

## 7. Approve and wait

When the user agrees in the next message, `dv_story_approve` with `plan`, `version` (from its preview) and `user_approved: true` for each plan, then `dv_proj_wait`. Each approval reports the plan's `timeline`; the cut is the `asset` of that timeline's clip in the project summary.

## 8. Ranked review

If `dv_asset_grab_still` is in your tool list, call it once per still with `inputs.video` = the cut's asset and `at` = the middle second of each copy beat, then `last`, and look at each still; otherwise ask the user to check the copy and the color.

Write each problem as `t=4.2s · where · problem → exact change · what stays`, ranked. P0 breaks the ad: misspelled or doubled copy, a wrong color, a banned item, a wrong ratio. P1 weakens it: over 3 s with nothing new, a soft ending. P2 is polish. Never score the cut.

To fix a cut, update its plan and repeat steps 6 and 7; only the changed shot renders. Never render a plan's shot with `dv_shot_render` or lay it on a timeline by hand.

## 9. Deliver

`dv_timeline_export` with `timeline` = each plan's timeline. Reply with each link, ratio and length, the copy, the storylines not built, the open notes, and what nobody checked (you saw stills and heard no sound). Offer one next step once, such as another ratio.

## What this procedure cannot do

When the user asks for one of these, say so before planning:

- Voice-over or music laid over the cut: no audio lane; the sound comes from the shot.
- Titles, captions, a logo or an end card over the picture: no text lane; the model draws the copy.
- Reframing one render into another ratio: each ratio renders again.
- A product picture made from the photo: the image model takes text only.
- One approval for several plans: each plan gets its own call.
